## 2. Non-negotiable acceptance and fidelity rules

An original obligation receives credit only for a recorded sound retirement as unused, or for a proof preserving the required statement with:

1. Exact approval of every admitted dependency.
2. Proved nondefinitional project translations, including actual maps, sections, scalar structures, and universal evaluations.
3. Matching changed-module and reverse-dependency native evidence.
4. The relevant source, native type/universe, runtime, and dependency bindings.

A helper, proof split, copied statement, namespace change, moved admission, source-only review, conditional theorem, or successful diagnostic adds no unit. Namespace `PublishedInputs` does not establish eligibility.

A permitted literature input must state a precise known generic published theorem with its actual hypotheses and normalized canonical maps. Do not rename a composite project theorem into an admitted “generic” leaf, or admit the desired project comparison as an input. Scope restrictions such as arbitrary versus locally Noetherian tests, schemes versus algebraic spaces, sheaf epimorphism versus every-open section surjectivity, and base-field/residue compatibility must remain explicit.

The native walker traverses types, opaque proof bodies, inductive constructors, and all referenced modules. A `#print axioms` result showing `sorryAx` alone does not identify the direct admitted leaves. Every such leaf needs the exhaustive walk and full source/native type review. Standard foundations are `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`; `sorryAx` is permitted only through separately approved actual published leaves.

Cache reuse requires exact source/imports, direct dependency output bytes, recursive source ancestry, runtime, flags, output, receipt, and log bindings. An ancestor source change invalidates descendants even when an intermediate output happens to be byte-identical. Do not relabel a diagnostic output as a stock output or a moved module's old receipt as a check of its new source.

Use the union of old/new import edges to recompute affected reverse cones. Keep ordered reversible source patches, full written headers and enclosing scopes, then compare elaborated native types. Preserve failed attempts and their actual scope; following a tactic marker is not a completed kernel check.
