# OBL-052: a seven-declaration source-order cut

## Recommendation

Move the original two-point dominance theorem and its six same-file consumers from `RecompileExtensionsPart02` into later modules. Keep the remaining Ext02 declarations in their current order. This avoids moving the generic dual-thickening and deformation infrastructure out of its existing modules.

This is a **read-only, statically checked plan**. No extraction, production edit, native acceptance, or source-order proof is claimed. The checked conditional dominance prototype still takes four generic criteria explicitly; those criteria must be discharged from their reviewed inputs before an unconditional production replacement is installed.

## Why this is smaller than lowering the foundations

Directly importing the new dominance proof into Ext02 is cyclic: for example, `LSNodalTangent` imports `LSCotangentDeformations`, which imports `LSHomVelocity`, which reaches Ext02 through `LSDualThickeningNormalForm` and `LSFiniteFreeBaseChange`.

One possible solution would extract ScalarSections, Construction's scalar/pullback core, Infinitesimal, generic FamilyVariation, marked-evaluation maps, and generic variation from several files. The seven-declaration cut avoids that broader refactor. It also avoids the separate dependency of `Charts.tangent_finite` on `Refinement.Jets.PointFrame`, which prevents blindly moving the whole Construction tail before Refinement.
